WHAT IS REAL · WHAT IS SIMULATED

Real data. Elevation: USGS/JPL Mars 2020 CTX DEM mosaic, 20 m. Thermal inertia: Edwards & Buz 2021 (THEMIS). Geology: Sun & Stack 2020, USGS SIM 3464. Minerals: Parente, Itoh & Saranathan 2021 and Horgan et al. 2020 (CRISM). Climate: published MEDA results from Perseverance; clock and season from the NASA PDS MEDA catalogue. Moons: NASA/JPL Horizons, live when the service can be reached.

Simulated. Everything smaller than one map pixel in the game (rocks, ripples, soil patches, wheel–soil behaviour), the alert-level radiation events, the minigames and the story. Quiet radiation level: Curiosity's RAD in Gale crater (Hassler et al. 2014), because Perseverance carries no radiation detector.
